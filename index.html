WHY FUNNO EXISTS

Learning works better when everyone feels connected.

In a busy classroom, assignments can get scattered, practice can feel repetitive, and it can be hard for a teacher to spot who needs a little help. Funno brings learning activities, progress, and teacher support into one shared space.

01 · THE CHALLENGE

Practice can lose its spark

When learning is only a list of tasks, students may struggle to stay curious or know what to try next.

02 · THE TEACHER’S VIEW

Progress needs a clear home

Teachers need one place to share lessons and activities, review results, and offer feedback to their learners.

03 · THE FUNNO APPROACH

Learn, play, and get support

Teachers create quizzes, assignments, and playful challenges. Students use their teacher’s code to reach that teacher’s learning space.

HOW IT WORKS

One simple path into learning.

  1. Teacher createsA private studio and a code to share.
  2. Student joinsSign in, enter the teacher’s code, and connect to that studio.
  3. Learning continuesTry activities, see results, and ask the teacher for help.
